Frequently Asked Questions

What is What U Want Rentals?

What U Want Rentals is a self-drive vehicle rental service that provides you with all the privacy, fun and convenience of your own vehicle, without the hassles of owning and maintaining one.

Am I eligible to use What U Want Rentals?

To avail our services, you must be at least 18 years old and possess a valid original driving license. Digital copies or A4 prints are not accepted.

Is there a "Kilometres limit"?

Yes, most plans have a limit (usually 100-300 Kms per day). Driving beyond this limit attracts additional charges per kilometre.

In case of an accident, what is my liability?

Your liability is typically limited to a maximum of Rs. 50,000 for damages, subject to compliance with our User Agreement and safety rules.

How do I make a booking?

You can book via our website, mobile app, or by calling our support line. Payment is required in advance.
