Refund Policy

This Refund Policy applies to all rentals and transactions conducted with M/S What U Want Rentals (hereinafter referred to as "WUW Rentals"), a proprietorship registered in Udupi district of Karnataka State. Please read this policy carefully before completing any booking or payment.

⚠️
All Sales Are Final — No Refunds WUW Rentals does not offer refunds on any rental payments, deposits, or fees once a booking is confirmed.

No Refund Policy

WUW Rentals operates a strict no-refund policy. All payments made toward vehicle rentals — including but not limited to rental fees, security deposits, advance payments, extension charges, and any other fees — are non-refundable once a booking has been confirmed.

By completing a booking and making payment, the User expressly acknowledges and agrees to this no-refund policy.

Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to all of the following, without exception:

  • Cancellations initiated by the User at any time after booking confirmation.
  • Early returns — no refund will be issued for unused rental days.
  • No-shows or failure to pick up the reserved vehicle.
  • Bookings cancelled due to the User's ineligibility (e.g., missing documents, license issues).
  • Security deposits withheld for damage, violations, or outstanding dues.
  • Payments made for extensions, upgrades, or additional services.

Security Deposits

Security deposits collected at the time of booking or vehicle pickup are held against potential damages, traffic violations, fuel discrepancies, late returns, or any outstanding dues. The deposit amount or any portion thereof may be forfeited in accordance with WUW Rentals' terms and conditions. No portion of the security deposit is guaranteed to be returned.

Vehicle Unavailability

In the rare event that WUW Rentals is unable to provide the booked vehicle due to circumstances solely within WUW Rentals' control (e.g., mechanical failure with no suitable substitute available), WUW Rentals may, at its sole discretion, offer a credit toward a future booking or an alternate vehicle. This does not constitute a right to a cash refund.

Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising from this Refund Policy shall be governed by the laws of India and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Udupi, Karnataka. Disputes may also be referred to arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
