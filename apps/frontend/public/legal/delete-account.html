Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your WUW Rentals account and the personal data associated with it.

Option 1 — Delete from the app

The fastest way is directly in the WUW Rentals app:

  1. Open the WUW Rentals app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your password and type DELETE.

Your account is deleted immediately once confirmed.

Option 2 — Request without the app

If you can no longer access the app, send us a deletion request from the email address registered on your account. We verify ownership before deleting, and complete verified requests within 30 days.

Note: If you have an active or upcoming booking, please complete or cancel it first. An account cannot be deleted while a rental is open.

What is deleted

The following personal data is permanently erased:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your postal address and date of birth
  • All KYC documents, including driving licence images
  • Your password and any linked Google sign-in

What is retained, and why

Some records cannot be erased on request because Indian tax and accounting law requires us to keep them. These are retained in a form that is no longer linked to your personal details.

Data Reason Retention
Booking records Proof of contract and service delivered 8 years
Invoices, receipts and credit notes Statutory books of account 8 years
Payment and refund records Financial reconciliation and audit 8 years
Damage and incident reports Insurance and legal claims Until the claim is closed

Once these retention periods expire, the remaining records are deleted in the normal course of business.
